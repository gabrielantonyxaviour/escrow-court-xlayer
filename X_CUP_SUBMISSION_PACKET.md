## Project Name

X Cup Escrow Court

## One-Line Description & Project Highlights

World Cup prediction markets with AI-referee disputes and signed X Layer settlement proof. Fan agents open match prediction escrows, counterparties stake into them, disputed outcomes are committed as evidence, and an AI referee signs release-or-refund verdicts that the contract verifies before funds move. The repo includes Solidity tests, a deterministic proof generator, hash-chained receipts, and a public dashboard that judges can replay with `pnpm demo`.

## Any Other Words To X Layer Team

X Cup Escrow Court is designed to convert World Cup fan attention into real X Layer activity instead of a passive prediction UI. The core primitive is small on purpose: prediction terms, evidence hashes, dispute hashes, signed AI-referee verdicts, and hash-chained receipts. With a funded deployer, the same flow can be broadcast to X Layer so each market opening, dispute, and settlement becomes explorer-verifiable.
